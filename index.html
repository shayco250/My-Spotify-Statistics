    How do I get my listening history from Spotify?
    1. Open Spotify → Account → Privacy settings.
    2. Tick Extended streaming history and request the download.
    3. Spotify emails you a zip within a few days. Unzip it.
    4. Upload every Streaming_History_Audio_*.json file from that folder here.

    The smaller Account data export only covers the last year and does not contain enough detail for this report.

        How the score works
        • Spread (40%) — how many separate days you played it, measured against how long you have known it.
        • Volume (35%) — total plays on a log scale, so a new favourite is not buried by an old giant.
        • Recency (25%) — decays quickly once you stop reaching for it.
